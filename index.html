FILE 001 / THE STORY

SO, WHAT'S
THE STORY?

There's something strange going on... ✦

Welcome to X-EMPLAR! School of Heroes, a school where becoming a true hero means mastering the five pillars of health: nutrition, exercise, wellbeing, sleep, and social !

But something strange has been happening around campus. Food is coming alive, rooms are getting closed off, teachers are disappearing, and it’s up to the heroes-in-training to figure it out.

To level up your health and heroism, you must explore, play minigames, and make connections to reveal the truth!
